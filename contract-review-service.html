FAQ

What buyers usually ask first

Is this legal advice?

No. We prepare documentation packets with clause indexing and risk flags. Legal strategy and advice come from licensed counsel.

How fast do I get the packet?

Standard turnaround is 3 business days. Rush service is available for an added fee.

What kinds of contracts do you review?

Service agreements, vendor contracts, MSAs, SOWs, freelance and consulting engagements, and commercial contracts for U.S. SMBs.

Can you negotiate on my behalf?

No. Documentation only. We flag the risks; you or your attorney handle negotiation.

Do you work with clients in any state?

Yes — documentation packets are U.S.-wide. State-specific contract-law nuances (if any) are confirmed at intake; engagements are remote.
