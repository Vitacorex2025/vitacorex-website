Revenue Recovery Workflow Design

Reduce recoverable leakage before outside escalation begins.

VitaCoreX deploys a structured pre-agency recovery workflow for B2B and healthcare-facing portfolios: behavioral segmentation, controlled offers, guarantor activation where supported, ACH-first payment discipline, and litigation-ready documentation. Executive Snapshot

The problem is usually sequencing, not just collections. Immediate contingency escalation compresses retained recovery, while unstructured payment attempts create weak documentation, higher dispute risk, and lower predictability.

Where leakage starts

Aged balances decay. Informal repayment attempts lack agreement discipline. ACH is not enforced early enough. Guaranties are not activated systematically. Leadership lacks comparable KPI visibility.

Behavioral segmentation

Accounts are categorized by aging, payment history, responsiveness, documentation status, and guarantor exposure so escalation becomes controlled and repeatable.

Conditional offer conversion

Offer windows are policy-controlled and time-bound so balances move into documented commitments instead of indefinite informal promises.

Guarantor activation

If a valid guaranty exists, activation and documented demand move earlier. If not, onboarding redesign becomes part of the recommendation.

ACH-first discipline

Recurring ACH at agreement execution reduces card churn, supports cure/default mechanics, and improves time-to-cash discipline.

Litigation-ready documentation

Agreements, notices, payment history, communications, and evidence packaging are built with downstream enforceability and cleaner counsel handoff in mind.

KPI dashboard

Offer acceptance, ACH activation, cure rate, net recovery, roll-to-agency, complaint rate, DSO compression, and evidence completeness are tracked during the pilot.
