Where revenue leakage starts

Recoverable balances leak when escalation is immediate, repayment attempts are informal, auto-pay is weak, and documentation quality remains low under stress.

  • Immediate contingency leakage
  • Weak payment-plan controls
  • Low ACH adoption
  • Weak evidence layer
  • Higher complaint risk

How the framework works

The operating model starts with itemization, consent, and clear terms, then moves through structured offers, signed agreements, auto-pay activation, monitoring, default triggers, and controlled escalation.

  • Behavioral segmentation
  • Conditional offer conversion
  • Guarantor activation
  • ACH-first discipline
  • Default and acceleration logic

Litigation-ready documentation

The documentation layer is built to strengthen enforceability, preserve the audit trail, and improve the quality of later handoff if internal sequencing fails.

  • Itemization retention
  • Agreement and ACH logs
  • Default notices
  • Communication history
  • Cure / roll-to-agency outcome tracking

Compliance posture

This offering is workflow design, documentation control, and escalation structuring. It is not a law-firm substitute and it should be deployed with counsel-aware guardrails.

  • FDCPA / Regulation F guardrails
  • Florida FCCPA awareness
  • E-SIGN and Florida UETA logic
  • Counsel-aware deployment
