Not a debt collector

VitaCoreX does not act as a debt collector and does not contact consumers, patients, subscribers, or debtors under the VitaCoreX name. We build and document operator-controlled pre-collection workflows, payment-resolution scripts, file readiness, escalation criteria, and handoff packets.
