Executive framing

This is an operating-control system for margin protection, documentation quality, and cash velocity—not a generic collections pitch.

How pilot proof is established

Pilot evidence should come from operator baselines, documented process changes, and measured file-quality improvements rather than illustrative public claims.

Framework components

  • Guarantor activation
  • ACH-first discipline

What leadership monitors

  • Balances converted before agency escalation
  • Documented payment commitments and cure rates
  • Exception queues, follow-up discipline, and aging movement
  • File quality improvements that reduce downstream cleanup
