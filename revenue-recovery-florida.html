Florida FCCPA note

The Florida Consumer Collection Practices Act (FCCPA) regulates licensed debt-collection activity. VitaCoreX consulting stays upstream of that line — we perform diagnostic, workflow, and pre-collection operator-side work, so FCCPA does not apply to our engagement. When licensed collection is warranted, we refer to partner agencies who operate under FCCPA and federal FDCPA.

FAQ

What operators usually ask first

Are you a collection agency?

No. VitaCoreX is a consulting firm. We work pre-collection. Licensed collection is referred to partner agencies.

Does FCCPA apply to your work?

FCCPA regulates licensed collection activity. Our pre-collection consulting stays upstream of that boundary, so FCCPA does not apply to the consulting engagement. If licensed collection is needed, it is referred out.

How are retainers priced?

Weekly, not monthly. Tier 1 $1,500/wk, Tier 2 $3,250/wk, Tier 3 $4,500/wk. Scoped and fixed before start.

Which states do you serve?

We serve operators across the United States. Florida-originated discipline preserved as a specialty; multi-state portfolios are standard scope; state-specific compliance (e.g., licensing, bond) is confirmed at intake.

Consulting success fees apply to recovery work and are distinct from contingency collection fees. Licensed collection is referred to partner agencies operating under FCCPA and FDCPA.
