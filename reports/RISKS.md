# Risks

## Blocker risk

- Deploy/runtime files still target the stale DocketMint/Node stack instead of the VitaCoreX runtime.

## Major risks

- Live analytics/Search Console verification still requires credentials and production access.
- The broader ranking strategy is not complete until the content-cluster and outreach layers are built.

## Minor risks

- Public pages still rely on a JS/localStorage shell handoff before the full runtime takes over.
- WebKit coverage is a spot check only.
- Utility/app routes remain outside the public blocker set by scope.
