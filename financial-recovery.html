About the retainer

How we work

Your team, your name, our method

  • Your own team contacts debtors in your company’s name; we design the method, write the scripts and documents, and train and check the team.
  • Debtors pay your company directly; your money never passes through us.
  • Prices and the rate are agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Accounts that need court move to small claims as a separate service, under your company’s power of attorney.
  • Business-to-business invoices only.

Questions

Do you take a percentage of what is recovered?

Only for the full rollout after the pilot: from the second month, and only of the increase. 12% (18% on invoices more than a year overdue) of the money that comes in above your usual level after we joined. The analysis and the first month have flat prices, or are free for retainer clients.

Which invoices fit?

Business-to-business invoices up to about two years old, backed by a contract, an order or a delivery record.

What does the retainer promotion include?

A free receivables analysis and a free first month of Financial Recovery. After the pilot, the full rollout costs the same share of the increase in net recovery. Mailing, service and fees are paid at cost; a debtor search your company orders and pays for itself.

What do I get at the end of the first month?

Weekly checks of your team’s work and a net-recovery report against the baseline: what came in, what was promised and which accounts stayed silent. Then you decide whether to continue.

What is paid separately?

Letters by certified mail (about $11 each), hand delivery or service on the debtor (Florida sheriff $40, private server about $40–$100) and any state or court fees, all at cost. A debtor search (skip tracing) is usually needed: your company orders and pays for it directly with a search service; we do not provide or pay for it.

Who talks to the debtors?

Your own team, in your company’s name. We set up the method, write the scripts and letters, show which documents to send and sign, train the team to collect evidence for small claims, and check the work every week.

How is the increase measured?

In the analysis we fix your baseline: average monthly recovery on overdue invoices for the 3 months before we joined. Each month our share is calculated only on what comes in above that baseline, from your own payment records.
