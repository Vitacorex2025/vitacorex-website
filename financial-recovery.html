How we work

Your name, your money, our process

  • Reminders and demand letters are issued in your company’s name and approved by you before they go out.
  • Debtors pay your company directly; your money never passes through us.
  • Flat prices for each step, agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Accounts that need court move to small claims under your company’s power of attorney.

Questions

Do you take a percentage of what is recovered?

No. Every step has a flat price, so you keep 100% of each payment.

Which invoices fit?

Business-to-business invoices up to about two years old, backed by a contract, an order or a delivery record.

What does the free monthly analysis include?

An updated aging report, which accounts moved, and the next step for each one: a reminder, a payment plan or small claims.

What do I get at the end of the pilot?

A weekly report during the 30 days and a final summary: what was paid, what was promised, and which accounts are ready for small claims, with the packets prepared.
