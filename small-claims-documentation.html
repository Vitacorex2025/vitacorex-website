      Questions

      Will you appear in court for my company?

      No. Who may represent a company in small claims depends on the state and court — usually an owner, an officer or an attorney. We prepare the documents and coordinate the administrative steps that are permitted.

      Which states do you cover?

      We review each state and court individually before quoting. This business service is not offered for New York.

      Are court fees included?

      No. Court filing fees and service of process are paid separately and listed in your quote.

      Do you guarantee the outcome?

      No. We make sure your case is clearly organized and documented; the decision belongs to the court.
