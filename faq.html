Revenue Recovery & Pre-Collection

What is pre-collection and how does it differ from traditional debt collection?

Pre-collection is the upstream revenue-recovery stage that runs before accounts are placed with a collection agency. VitaCoreX does not act as a debt collector and does not contact consumers, patients, subscribers, or debtors under the VitaCoreX name. We design and document operator-controlled pre-collection workflows, payment-resolution scripts, file readiness, escalation criteria, and handoff packets. Customer contact remains with the operator, its counsel, or a properly licensed collection provider where required. Operators running these workflows typically see higher net recovery than post-charge-off placement (industry benchmarks: 35-55 cents per dollar at pre-collection stage vs 10-14 cents at traditional-agency stage) with customer relationships preserved.

How does VitaCoreX improve net recovery rates?

VitaCoreX designs structured pre-collection workflows that the operator runs: evidence-based documentation, compliance-ready file hygiene, segmentation logic, escalation criteria, and script frameworks used by the operator’s own AR team (or its licensed vendor). VitaCoreX does not contact consumers, patients, subscribers, or debtors under the VitaCoreX name. Operators running these workflows typically see lower Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), fewer bad-debt write-offs, and preserved customer relationships because outreach stays branded and on-policy.

What is DSO and why does it matter?

DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) measures the average number of days it takes to collect payment after a sale. A high DSO means money is tied up in unpaid invoices, hurting cash flow. VitaCoreX helps businesses reduce DSO through pre-collection workflows, systematic follow-up, and optimized billing processes. Lower DSO means faster cash flow and healthier financials.

Is VitaCoreX a collection agency?

No. VitaCoreX is not a licensed collection agency and does not collect debts, take assignment of receivables, hold client funds, or contact debtors as a third-party collector. We support internal workflow design, documentation control, portfolio segmentation, file readiness, and escalation packets. Any regulated collection activity must be handled by the client, counsel, or a licensed collection provider where required. Workflows are FDCPA / Reg F-aware and subject to counsel or licensed-provider review when operator policy requires it — not a legal or compliance guarantee.

Do you contact patients, subscribers, customers, or debtors?

No, not as VitaCoreX and not as a third-party collector. Customer-facing communication remains with the operator, counsel, or a licensed provider. VitaCoreX may help prepare scripts, trackers, documentation standards, and escalation rules for client review — but the actual outreach stays under the operator’s own brand, policy, and regulatory footprint.
