FAQ

What buyers usually ask first

Is this legal advice?

No. Documentation review only. We flag terms; legal strategy and advice come from licensed counsel.

Can I get this same-day before I sign?

Rush review is available same-day for a flat +$49 add-on. Submit before noon ET and the packet is delivered by close of business. Standard (no add-on) turnaround is 2 business days.

What does the review actually cover?

Financing terms, APR and payment schedule, add-ons and fees, warranty and service-contract clauses, trade-in credit, and any non-standard clauses in the packet.

I already signed. Can you still review it?

Yes. Post-signature review documents exactly what you agreed to and flags issues you may want to raise with counsel.

Will you negotiate for me?

No. Documentation only. We flag concerns; you negotiate, or you engage an attorney.
