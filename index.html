Pre‑Collection Program (Patent Pending)

A documentation‑first workflow that increases recovery probability before litigation by enforcing a consistent, compliance‑aware intake and escalation structure.

1Scope & policy alignment

Define what is “collectible,” when to escalate, what evidence is required, and who approves each step.

2Case intake standard

Standard data schema: parties, venue/jurisdiction, contract terms, invoices, payment history, contacts, and prior attempts.

3Demand package & timeline

Template demand letters, documentation bundles, and a timeline that preserves optionality for counsel.

4Escalation handoff

When needed: litigation‑ready packaging and clean transfer to counsel/filing team with consistent labeling.
