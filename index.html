      Questions

      Before you start

      How do I know the final price?

      Fixed-price services such as online notarization and company formation show the full service fee before you pay; state and government fees are listed separately. For translations, apostilles, tax returns and document tasks we confirm the final price in writing after a short review — you pay only after you accept it.

      Do you give legal advice or go to court?

      No. VitaCoreX is not a law firm. We prepare and organize documents at your direction and handle administrative steps. If you need a lawyer, we can pass a short summary to an independent attorney — only with your consent.

      Which languages do you work in?

      We serve clients in English, Russian and Spanish. For translations, send any language pair: our coordinator confirms a qualified translator, the price and the date before you order.

      Will my document be accepted?

      Florida online notarizations and certified translations are widely used, but the office that receives your document decides what it accepts. Check its requirements first — we help you ask the right questions.

      How do I send documents safely?

      Not through the website form. After we confirm the scope, we send you a private upload link. Never send Social Security numbers, bank details or ID scans by email or through a form.

      Do you work outside Florida?

      Most services are remote across the U.S. Company formation and apostilles depend on the state involved. Small-claims support for companies depends on the state and court and is not offered for New York.

      Not sure what you need?

      Describe your task in a few lines. We reply with the right service, the price or the next step.
