The market problem

Problem → operating fix → proof

Law-firm economics are heavy

Florida lawyer rates average about $351/hour, and civil litigation averages about $322/hour before document chaos, delays, and rework are priced in.

High-volume work is misrouted

Companies often send routine, repeatable, or document-heavy tasks into expensive channels that were designed for advice and representation, not operational cleanup.

Weak intake destroys value

If chronology, exhibits, payor data, or contract support are incomplete, every later stage becomes slower, more expensive, and harder to defend.
