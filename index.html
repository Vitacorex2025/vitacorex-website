    Before you start

    Have another question? Call us on any weekday.

    (888) 794-8292
    How do I know the final price?

    Online notarization and company formation have fixed prices that you see before paying; state and government fees are shown on a separate line. For translations, apostilles, tax returns and other documents, we take a quick look first and send a written quote. You pay only once you agree to it.

    Do you handle court cases?

    Yes. We prepare court packets and Florida court forms from your answers, and run small-claims cases for companies by the hour under a power of attorney. If a matter needs an attorney, we tell you plainly.

    Which languages do you work in?

    We work with clients in English, Russian and Spanish. For translations, send any language pair: our coordinator checks which qualified translator can take it and gives you the price and date before you order.

    Will my document be accepted?

    That's always up to the office receiving it. Florida online notarizations and certified translations are common practice, but check the recipient's requirements first. We'll tell you what to ask.

    How do I send documents?

    Once we have agreed on the work, we send you a private upload link, usually the same day.

    Do you work outside Florida?

    Yes. Most services are online, across the U.S. For company formation and apostilles, it depends on the state involved. Small-claims work for companies depends on the state and court, and we don't take business cases in New York.

    Tell us in your own words

    You don't need to know what the service is called. We'll reply with what fits and what it costs.
