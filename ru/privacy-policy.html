Privacy Policy

Privacy for a company-first advisory site.

This policy explains how VitaCoreX handles public-site requests, optional attachments, consent settings, and structured lead-routing metadata.

What is collected

Public forms may collect contact details, company context, attribution data, language, page path, selected service line, and optional file attachments.

Why it is collected

Why it is collected

The data is used to evaluate fit, route the inquiry, store an audit-ready lead record, and coordinate the appropriate next step.

How it is stored

How it is stored

Submissions are stored in structured backend records with attachment metadata and optional CRM webhook delivery when configured.

What is not promised

What is not promised

The public site does not claim regulated clinical intake, legal representation, or any use beyond the bounded review and routing process described here.

Use the visible consent settings and browser controls when you want to limit optional measurement.

Essential storage supports routing, language choice, and form integrity.
