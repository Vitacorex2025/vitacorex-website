Who it is for

Operators who need a clean first pass before they send materials, spend on cleanup, or lose time deciding which workstream comes first.

  • Founders
  • Operators
  • Finance leads
  • General managers
  • Decision support

What the intake should produce

The output should identify the most likely service line, what documents to send now, the response window, and the next disciplined step.

  • Recommended service line
  • Estimated fit
  • What to send now
  • Expected response window
  • Recommended next step

What documents to send now

The fastest path is usually a short chronology plus 2–5 anchor documents: agreements, invoices, notices, screenshots, and the latest communication thread.

  • Chronology
  • Agreement
  • Invoices
  • Notices / letters
  • Screenshots / thread

What happens after submission

After the first pass, the work should move into one primary lane: legal file control, revenue recovery workflow design, or a tighter packet-build engagement.

  • Route to primary service line
  • Clarify priority
  • Define first work product
  • Set response timing
