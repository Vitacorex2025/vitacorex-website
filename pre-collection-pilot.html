Important operating boundary

VitaCoreX does not take assignment of receivables, does not hold client funds, does not collect payments under its own name, does not send legal demands, and does not contact consumers or debtors as a third-party collector.

Success fees compensate workflow design, documentation control, portfolio segmentation, escalation readiness, internal recovery infrastructure, and client-side operating support.

Any direct collection activity, legal demand, litigation, or regulated communication must be handled by the client, counsel, or a licensed provider where required.

Structured as a pre-collection consulting success fee, not a contingency collection fee. Licensed collection is referred to partner agencies when appropriate.

US Chamber benchmark for pre-collection recovery: 25–33%. VitaCoreX success fees are 25% below common benchmarks.

FAQ

What operators usually ask first

Is there really $0 upfront?

Yes, for approved portfolios only. Compensation is a success fee on eligible recovered cash during the pilot window. If your portfolio does not qualify, the Revenue Recovery Diagnostic ($1,500) and the Paid Workflow Pilot (from $2,500/mo) are the transparent paid alternatives.

What if nothing is recovered?

You owe nothing on the Qualified Net Recovery Pilot. No retainer, no setup. (This applies only to approved portfolios. Paid Workflow Pilot and Diagnostic engagements carry their published fees regardless of recovery outcome.)

Are you a collection agency?

No. VitaCoreX is a consulting firm. Licensed collection is referred to partner agencies when appropriate.

Are you a law firm?

No. We provide documentation, preparation, and workflow services. Legal strategy and advice are handled by licensed counsel.

How long until we see results?

Diagnostic in 7 days; first recovery cohort typically 30–45 days; full pilot report at day 90.illustrative
