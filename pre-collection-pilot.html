FAQ

What operators usually ask first

Is this really free?

Yes, for qualified portfolios. You only pay a success fee on cash we helped recover during the pilot window.

What if nothing is recovered?

You owe nothing. No retainer, no setup.

Are you a collection agency?

No. VitaCoreX is a consulting firm. Licensed collection is referred to partner agencies when appropriate.

Are you a law firm?

No. We provide documentation, preparation, and workflow services. Legal strategy and advice are handled by licensed counsel.

How long until we see results?

Diagnostic in 7 days; first recovery cohort typically 30–45 days; full pilot report at day 90.
