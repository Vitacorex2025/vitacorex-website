# DocketMint Brand Rules

## Approved Brand

Use `DocketMint` as the product name.

Approved supporting label:

`DocketMint by VitaCoreX`

## Approved Positioning

Use this positioning sentence:

`DocketMint is one platform with two operational engines: Legal Workflow and Recovery Pipeline.`

## Naming Rules

1. External product name must be DocketMint.
2. Internal architecture docs should also use DocketMint as the active product name.
3. Legal Workflow and Recovery Pipeline are environment names, not brand names.
4. Capability modules may be named by function, but not presented as separate product brands.

## Banned Naming

Do not use these as active product names:

1. Deadline CRM
2. Deadline CRM Calendar
3. Case Generator
4. Generator App
5. Recovery App
6. Legal App

## Legacy Archive Rule

Legacy archive names, package names, and historical filenames may still contain `Deadline CRM` during transition. Those labels are legacy technical residue only and must not be promoted into live positioning, product copy, or future repo naming.

## UI And Copy Rules

1. Home should describe the platform as one system with two engines.
2. Recovery copy should stay vertically specific and not degrade into generic CRM language.
3. Legal copy should describe workflow and execution, not a disconnected courtroom product.
4. Generator-related copy should talk about document or packet generation as a feature inside DocketMint.
