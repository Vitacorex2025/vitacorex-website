# Ad Copy — Responsive Search Ads

Google RSA format: up to **15 headlines** (30 chars each) and **4 descriptions** (90 chars each). Google mixes & matches automatically; we pin Headline 1 and Headline 2 to lock in brand messaging.

**Pin strategy for all ads:**
- Headline 1 (pinned to position 1): service-specific title
- Headline 2 (pinned to position 2): brand or phone
- Headlines 3–15: flexible (Google optimizes)
- Description 1: primary value prop
- Descriptions 2–4: flexible

**Final URL:** per-campaign (see below)
**Display path:** `/VitaCoreX/<service>`
