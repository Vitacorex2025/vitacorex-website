# Google Ads — RSA Ad Copy (Russian)

**Purpose:** Russian-language Responsive Search Ads for VitaCoreX private-client campaigns. Clone of the English `ad-copy.md` structure, translated for a Russian-speaking US audience.

**When to use:** When you scale past the $50/mo test budget and want to capture RU-speaking searchers (FL, NY, CA, WA diaspora). Not needed for the current $50/mo English-only launch.

**Constraints (Google Ads RSA):**
- Headlines: **≤ 30 characters** each — max 15 per ad
- Descriptions: **≤ 90 characters** each — max 4 per ad
- Character counts in Russian differ from English; budget ~1.6× for Cyrillic. Every headline/description below is pre-checked under 30/90 chars respectively.

**Landing pages:** RU copy auto-renders on the EN URLs when user's browser locale is RU or the user clicks the RU toggle. Final URLs stay English (`/private-intake-immigration.html` etc.) — no separate `/ru/` paths needed.

**Pin strategy:**
- Pin H1 (service name) to **position 1**
- Pin H2 (phone number) to **position 2**
