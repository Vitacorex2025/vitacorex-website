# Google Ads — RSA Ad Copy (Spanish)

**Purpose:** Spanish-language Responsive Search Ads for VitaCoreX private-client campaigns. Mirrors the English `ad-copy.md` structure, translated for a US Hispanic audience (FL, TX, CA, NY, IL).

**When to use:** When you scale past the $50/mo test budget and want to capture Spanish-speaking searchers. The ES market in the US is large across all 3 verticals — expect lower CPCs than English on many keywords.

**Constraints (Google Ads RSA):**
- Headlines: **≤ 30 characters** each — max 15 per ad
- Descriptions: **≤ 90 characters** each — max 4 per ad
- Spanish is ~10-15% longer than English on average; each headline/description below is manually checked under the 30/90 limits.

**Landing pages:** ES copy auto-renders on the EN URLs when user clicks the ES toggle. Final URLs stay English (`/private-intake-immigration.html` etc.).

**Pin strategy:** H1 (service name) pinned position 1; H2 (phone) pinned position 2.
