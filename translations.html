Questions

Does USCIS need a notarized translation?

No. USCIS asks for a complete English translation and a translator's certification that the translation is accurate and that the translator is competent. Notarization is an optional extra some other offices ask for.

How do I send the document?

After you accept the quote, we send a private upload link. Please do not attach documents to the request form or to email.

Do you translate rare languages?

Send any language pair. We confirm whether a qualified translator is available, the price and the date before you order. Rare languages may cost more.

Can I get a printed copy?

Yes. Printed copies and postage are quoted separately, line by line, before you pay.
