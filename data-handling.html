Confidentiality & data handling

How we handle your documents.

VitaCoreX is a documentation-control practice. Sensitive records flow through our hands at every engagement. The procedures below describe how we treat them — before, during, and after the work.

Engagement letter required for all matters. Mutual NDA available on request before first document exchange. Sensitive records coordinated through secure channels — never through public forms.

Document lifecycle

Intake

Initial documents are reviewed and classified. Public intake forms are designed for general business documents and non-regulated materials. For HIPAA-, attorney-, or regulator-sensitive records, request secure coordination before any transfer — we will provide an encrypted channel matched to the matter.

Active engagement

Documents are stored in access-controlled workspaces with audit logging. The principal has primary access to all engagement documents. Specialist resources receive scoped access per matter only — never blanket access.

Post-engagement

Documents are retained for the period specified in the engagement letter, then purged unless longer retention is required by law or by written client direction. Deletion-on-request: all client documents are removed within five business days of a written request, with a confirmation log.

Access & storage

  • All documents stored encrypted at rest.
  • Transfer via TLS only — public HTTP for sensitive material is never accepted.
  • Backup logs retained for the same period as the underlying records, then rotated.
  • No third-party analytics on document-content pages.
  • No cookies linking individual identity to engagement-specific documents.

Secure coordination options

For highly sensitive or regulated records, request secure coordination before document transfer. Available channels:

  • Encrypted email (PGP key on request)
  • Password-protected file-transfer link with single-use token
  • Client portal access (provisioned for active engagement only)

NDA language

Mutual NDA is available on request and is recommended before any document exchange tied to a specific matter. Standard form covers two-way confidentiality, scoped term, and document return / destruction obligations on engagement close.

Initial intake conversations are confidential by default — VitaCoreX does not disclose intake details to third parties without written client direction. Engagement is established only under a written engagement letter signed by both parties.

What VitaCoreX is — and is not

VitaCoreX LLC is a Florida-registered company providing remote administrative, workflow, documentation, and file-control support across the United States where permitted by applicable law. We are not a law firm, do not provide legal advice, are not a licensed collection agency, and do not collect debts. State-specific court, collection, privacy, immigration, and unauthorized-practice-of-law rules are confirmed before engagement.

Privileged communications cannot be promised by VitaCoreX. For attorney-client privilege, engage with licensed counsel directly; we work alongside counsel as a documentation resource.
