For Companies

Corporate advisory and recovery — priced by engagement shape, not by hour.

Three shapes operators actually use: a Qualified Net Recovery Pilot with $0 upfront for approved AR portfolios, a fixed-scope file-control engagement that cleans the matter packet, and a weekly retainer that runs continuous governance across locations. Operators who do not qualify for the success-fee pilot choose the Revenue Recovery Diagnostic ($1,500 fixed) or the Paid Workflow Pilot (from $2,500/mo).

First month at 50% off — qualified retainers only.

Tier B-1 · $0 upfront for approved portfolios

Qualified Net Recovery Pilot

$0 upfront for approved portfolios

Compensation is a consulting success fee only on eligible recovered cash, by AR age band. No retainer, no contingency lock-in.

Best for

U.S. companies with qualifying AR portfolios: ≥ $100K, 30–180 day aged balances, operator-side governance.

  • 7-day diagnostic of the portfolio
  • 90-day structured pilot on a live AR slice
  • Weekly KPI dashboard — measured against operator baseline
  • Executive deck at close
  • Success fee: 15% / 18% / 22.5% by AR age (0–90 / 91–180 / 181+ days)

Explicitly excludes: legal advice, licensed collection activity, consumer-facing third-party collection, court representation, payment custody.

Structured as a pre-collection consulting success fee, not a contingency collection fee. Licensed collection activity referred to partner agencies when appropriate.
