Auto Deal Integrity

Upload the deal documents, enter VIN and mileage, review honest source status, and generate a branded deal audit PDF.

How to use this service

Upload the deal documents, confirm the VIN and mileage, and review the output before you negotiate or sign. Nothing is sent automatically.
What to upload

Buyer order, retail installment contract, worksheet, finance sheet, or phone photos of the deal paperwork.

Optional supporting files

Listing URL, screenshots, advertised price, and any notes showing add-ons or fees.

What we check

VIN identity, recalls, observed pricing references, fee and add-on structure, suspicious price gaps, and honest provider availability.

What you receive

A branded VitaCoreX deal audit with pricing range logic, flagged add-ons, provider status, and next-step negotiation guidance.

Important limits

KBB, Edmunds, Carfax, and J.D. Power / NADA are shown honestly as provider slots unless a lawful live integration is configured.

Before you rely on it

Review trim, condition, history, and state-specific fee rules before making a final decision.
