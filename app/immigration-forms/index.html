Immigration Forms Studio

Upload the USCIS PDF, complete the guided intake, review missing evidence, and generate a draft PDF for review.

How to use this service

Upload the USCIS PDF first, then fill in the guided intake on this page. Nothing is filed or sent automatically.
What to upload

The PDF form you want to complete, plus any intake documents that help identify names, dates, A-number, passport data, and contact details.

Optional supporting files

Passport copies, civil documents, prior notices, and any intake worksheet you already have.

What we check

Fillable PDF fields, document-derived hints, missing required fields, and evidence prompts tied to the detected form type.

What you receive

A review-ready PDF draft with mapped values plus a visible checklist of missing fields and evidence still needed.

Important limits

This is form and evidence assembly only. It does not decide legal eligibility or replace attorney review.

Best use

Complete the guided intake, review the missing items, then download the draft and review it before filing.
