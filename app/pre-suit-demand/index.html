Pre Suit Demand

How to use this service

Upload the contract, invoices, ledger, and proof of work in the active matter first. This module turns them into a recovery path. Nothing is sent automatically.

What to upload

Contract or agreement, invoices, payment ledger or statements, emails, and proof of delivery or completion.

Optional supporting files

Personal guaranty, acceptance emails, change orders, or settlement history.

What we check

Supported balance, invoice count, contract protections, evidence gaps, and the most realistic next recovery path.

What you receive

Balance summary, evidence checklist, demand draft, and a recommended next step for recovery.

Important limits

This is a workflow and document engine, not a substitute for state-specific legal advice or filing review.

Best practice

Use this after you finish uploads in the matter so the packet can reflect the strongest available evidence set.
