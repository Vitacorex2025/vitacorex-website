Contracts & Risk

Contract Risk Scanner

This standalone workflow extracts clause findings from uploaded contract files and turns them into actionable risk commentary.

Best Contract Builder

How to use this service

Upload the contract, add supporting drafts if needed, run the scanner, and review the findings before negotiating or signing. Nothing is sent automatically.
What to upload

Contract, guaranty, master service agreement, SOW, addendum, or other agreement files.

Optional files

Redlines, prior drafts, counterparty comments, or supporting notes that help explain the business deal.

What we check

Strong clauses, weak clauses, missing protections, dangerous clauses, and practical drafting risk.

What you receive

Expanded commentary, fallback wording, legal-commercial rationale, and state-specific cautions where law can vary.

Important limits

This is a structured drafting review, not a guarantee that every clause is enforceable in every state or fact pattern.
