How it works

From request to filing

  1. Short request

    Tax year, type of return and state. No SSN, no tax forms at this step.

  2. What's included and the price

    Our accounting team confirms what is included and the price in writing.

  3. Secure documents

    You upload W-2s, 1099s and other papers through a private link.

  4. Review and filing

    We go through the return with you and file only after you approve it.

Questions

Can you help with a past year or an amendment?

Yes, tell us the year and what needs to change. Past-year and amended returns are quoted separately.

Why don't you ask for my SSN now?

To protect you. We collect identification numbers and tax documents only after we've agreed on the work and the price, and only through a secure channel.

Do you guarantee a refund?

No. The result depends on your income and documents. We prepare an accurate return and explain it to you before filing.

Which states do you cover?

Tell us your state in the request. We confirm whether we can prepare that state's return before quoting.
