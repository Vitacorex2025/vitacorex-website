What the report contains

Three lenses on the address: demographics, traffic, and zoning + permit history. Same dataset a national chain would build internally — in one document you can read in thirty minutes.

Demographics & population profile

Resident population, median household income, age distribution, and education mix at 1/3/5-mile radii. Daytime workers separated from residents. Niche indices for fitness, childcare, luxury, QSR.

Traffic & drive-time

Vehicle and pedestrian counts on the primary frontage with peak-hour and weekday/weekend patterns. Drive-time isochrones (5/10/15 min) mapped against resident and daytime population.

Zoning & permit history

Zoning designation + permitted-use list. Recent permit history on the parcel and immediate neighbors. Flags for conditional-use, historic overlays, sign-code, parking minimums.

Competitive landscape

Drive-time competitive map (not straight-line radius), white-space and cannibalization check, and up to two comparable sites analyzed on the same dimensions.

Competitive map

Direct competitors at 1/3/5-mile drive-time bands, plus adjacent-category operators (same daypart / same spend) called out separately. Annotated with category, footprint, and 24-month open/close signals.

White-space & cannibalization

Gaps in the competitive map (categories or price points missing in the trade area). Explicit cannibalization risk where the address sits inside an existing operator's trade area.

Comparable sites

Up to two comparable addresses analyzed on the same dimensions — demographics, traffic, zoning, competition — so the decision is made against real alternatives.

What it's used for

Pre-lease decision support, lease-negotiation source material, and board / lender review document — the format expected by SBA, franchisor, and multi-member governance.

Pre-lease decision

Read before signing the LOI or lease. Identifies the 2–3 items most likely to change rent offer or kill the deal: zoning, permit-history red flags, saturation, demographic mismatch.

Lease negotiation

Zoning + permit findings become negotiating points: landlord variance responsibilities, lease contingencies on permit approvals, rent abatement during build-out delays.

Board & lender review

For multi-member LLCs, franchisee approvals, and SBA loans — the document board members and lenders are already trained to read. No explanation needed.

Process

Five stages from intake to final delivery. One round of clarification included; report ships within 7–10 business days.

Address intake

20-min scoping call: subject address, category, target customer, up to two comparables. We confirm what's answerable from available data.

Data collection

Census/ACS demographics, public-GIS zoning + permits, commercial traffic where published, category-specific indices.

Competitive mapping

Direct + adjacent operators at drive-time bands; footprint and open/close signals from public filings + local reporting.

Draft report

Commented PDF draft. One round of clarification — questions or specific lenses (weekend foot traffic, evening safety context, sign-code).

Final delivery

Final PDF with comparables, map exhibits, and citations appendix. Delivered within 7–10 business days of intake.

VitaCoreX LLC prepares location-analysis documentation for internal decision support. We are not a licensed real-estate broker, appraiser, or environmental consultant. Broker representation, formal appraisals, environmental site assessments (Phase I/II ESA), and zoning-variance legal strategy remain with their respective licensed professionals. Our role is the pre-signature evidence package.
