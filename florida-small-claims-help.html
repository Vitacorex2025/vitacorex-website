Florida small-claims rules

Rule 7.050, $8,000 ceiling, pro-se-friendly

Florida’s small-claims system is designed to be accessible without counsel. Clean documentation makes it work.

Rule 7.050 basics

Florida Small Claims Rule 7.050 governs matters at or under $8,000. Filing is done at the county clerk’s office with the Statement of Claim.

Pro se friendly

You can represent yourself. The court expects clean exhibits, a clear chronology, and a one-page statement of what you’re asking for.

Counties covered

Primary: Hillsborough (Tampa) and Pinellas. Statewide Florida service is available by arrangement.

Above $8,000

Matters above the small-claims ceiling move to county civil or circuit civil. Tier 2 Attorney-Ready Civil Packet is built for those.

FAQ

What Florida claimants usually ask first

Is this legal advice?

No. Documentation and preparation only. Florida Bar rules on the Unauthorized Practice of Law apply.

Will you file my claim?

No. You or your attorney file. We prepare the packet so filing is clean.

What is the Florida small-claims ceiling?

Florida Small Claims Rule 7.050 covers claims at or under $8,000. Larger matters move to civil divisions; Tier 2 is built for those.

Which counties do you serve?

Primary: Hillsborough (Tampa) and Pinellas. Statewide Florida service is available by arrangement.

What about the hearing day?

Hearing documentation support is available as an add-on at $110/hour — 1-hour minimum for consultation or remote advisory calls; 2-hour minimum for in-person or remote hearing attendance. We are not your legal representative; we support the documentation side only.
