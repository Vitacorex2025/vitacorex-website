Small-claims context

State-aware, pro-se-friendly, court-ready format

Every U.S. state runs a small-claims system designed to be accessible without counsel. The forms and ceilings differ; the documentation discipline is the same. We confirm your state’s specifics at intake.

State ceilings vary

Small-claims ceilings range from roughly $5,000 to $15,000 depending on the state. We confirm your state’s ceiling and form at intake; matters above the ceiling move to general civil divisions.

Pro se friendly

Small-claims courts in every state allow self-representation. They expect clean exhibits, a clear chronology, and a one-page statement of what you’re asking for.

Coverage

U.S.-wide remote service. Filing happens at your local county clerk’s office; we prepare the packet so filing is clean.

Above the small-claims ceiling

Matters above your state’s small-claims ceiling move to general civil or circuit civil divisions. Tier 2 Attorney-Ready Civil Packet is built for those.

What we do

Documentation packets for small-claims and civil matters, U.S.-wide

The filing step is yours (or your attorney’s). Getting there — chronology, exhibits, memo, court-ready format — is what we handle.

Chronology

Every event, date, and document pulled into a single timeline a court (or counsel) can follow.

Exhibit indexing

Every exhibit labeled, tabbed, and cross-referenced to the chronology. No scrambling in the hearing.

Gap & issue log

Weak or missing evidence flagged; issue memo prepared so next steps are obvious.

Counsel handoff format

Packet formatted so your attorney can review and act without re-organizing what you already gathered.

FAQ

What claimants usually ask first

Is this legal advice?

No. Documentation and preparation only. Your state’s bar rules on the Unauthorized Practice of Law apply.

Will you file my claim?

No. You or your attorney file. We prepare the packet so filing is clean.

What is the small-claims ceiling in my state?

Small-claims ceilings vary by state, typically $5,000–$15,000. We confirm your state’s ceiling at intake. Larger matters move to general civil divisions; Tier 2 is built for those.

Which states do you serve?

U.S.-wide remote service. Filing happens at your local county clerk’s office; we prepare the packet so filing is clean for your jurisdiction.
