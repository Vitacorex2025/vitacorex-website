What we check

The paperwork traps that cost buyers the most money.

We read the fine print for you and put the red flags in a single short checklist. You walk into the dealer (or sign the e-pad) knowing exactly what to push back on.

Hidden & duplicated fees

Dealer fees, doc fees, reconditioning, nitrogen, VIN etching, "market adjustment" — we flag which are negotiable, which are illegal, and which are duplicated across line items.

Finance rate vs. buy rate

We compare the rate on your contract against typical buy rates for your credit tier so you know if the dealer marked it up.

Add-ons audit

GAP, extended warranty, tire-and-wheel, maintenance packages, key replacement. We show the actual value vs. what you're charged and whether you can cancel post-signing.

Total cost breakdown

The actual out-the-door number vs. the advertised price. Tax, tag, title, doc, add-ons, financing charges — in one clean summary.

Cancellation windows

State-specific rules on what you can cancel after signing (GAP, warranty, unwinding the deal). We mark every deadline.

Frequently asked

Quick answers before you fill the form.

I'm at the dealership right now. Can you help today?

Yes, often. Pick "Same day" in the urgency field. We turn same-day reviews around in 2–4 hours during business hours. For after-hours or weekend deals, submit anyway — we'll tell you which specific numbers to push back on before you sign.

How much does a review cost?

Quick contract review starts at $89. Full breakdown (finance contract + add-ons + total-cost summary) is $149. Post-signing dispute review with cancellation pathway is $219. You see the scope quote before work starts.

Can you negotiate with the dealer for me?

No — we don't represent you. We give you a specific, written list of what to push back on (lines to ask the finance manager to remove, dollar amounts to dispute, questions that reveal rate markup). You deliver it. Most clients save 10–20× the review fee.

Already signed — can you still help?

Yes. Pick "Post-signing." Many states have cancellation windows for GAP, extended warranty, and the deal itself. We identify which apply in your state and what you need to send to cancel. If the dealer committed fraud, we refer you to a consumer-protection attorney.

Do you do pre-purchase inspections?

No mechanical inspection — we only review paperwork. For mechanical inspection we recommend a pre-purchase inspection by an independent shop. We can flag dealer-inspection gaps in the paperwork (e.g., no frame check, no diagnostic report).
