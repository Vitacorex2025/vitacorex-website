About

Operational support for cleaner workflow and cleaner files.

VitaCoreX helps companies improve recovery workflow, organize documents, and prepare cleaner packets for review. This site is intentionally simple and public-facing.

What VitaCoreX does

  • Reviews workflow structure before accounts move into higher-cost escalation.
  • Helps organize chronology, exhibits, and supporting records.
  • Clarifies what documents are missing before a handoff or review.

What VitaCoreX does not do

  • It is not a law firm.
  • It does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
  • It is not presented here as a licensed collection agency.

How this public site is meant to be used

The site explains the company, links to public resources, and gives a simple contact path. Public upload tools and paid flows were intentionally removed from this version so the website stays readable and low-risk.
