What happens next

  1. 1

    Within 2–10 minutes

    You receive a second email with your session invite link. Open it on any device with a working camera and microphone.

  2. 2

    Identity check

    The platform checks your government-issued photo ID and asks 5 knowledge-based questions (KBA). It takes about 2 minutes.

  3. 3

    Live video session

    You meet the Florida Notary Public by video. You sign, and the notary completes the electronic notarization. 10–15 minutes.

  4. 4

    Tamper-evident PDF

    The notarized document is emailed to you right after the session ends.

Have ready before you join

  • A valid, unexpired government photo ID (driver license or passport)
  • A device with a working camera and microphone
  • The document you want notarized (PDF or photo)
  • A quiet, well-lit place

If the session link has not arrived within 15 minutes, or you need to reschedule, email info@vitacorexllc.com with your Stripe order number.
