What's in the formation packet

Six documents that get a Florida LLC operational and counsel-ready. Florida is the example because Sunbiz filings are our most common request — packet structure works nationwide.

Articles of Organization

Foundational Sunbiz filing — name, registered agent, principal office, management structure. Florida $125 state filing fee paid directly.

Operating Agreement

Customized governance: ownership %, capital contributions, distributions, voting, transfers, dissolution. Simplified format for single-member LLCs.

EIN Application (SS-4)

IRS Form SS-4 prepared with correct entity classification and tax treatment — pass-through, S-Corp, or C-Corp election.

Registered Agent Designation

Required FL designation with physical street address. We prepare the form and explain options; we don't serve as the agent ourselves.

Sunbiz Annual Report

Required annual filing by May 1. $138.75 for LLCs / $150 for corps. We prepare and send a deadline reminder so you avoid the $400 late fee.

Business License Research

Checklist of likely county/city tax receipts, professional licenses, or industry permits across Hillsborough, Pinellas, Orange, Miami-Dade, Broward.

Entity type options

Three structures we prepare. Tax strategy is your CPA's call — we prepare the documents that match.

Standard Florida LLC

Default pass-through (disregarded entity for single-member, partnership for multi-member). Simplest structure; FL has no state income tax for individuals.

S-Corp Election (Form 2553)

Reduces self-employment tax by splitting income between salary + distributions. Most useful when net income is consistently above $40K–$50K/yr.

C-Corp Formation

Articles of Incorporation, bylaws, initial board resolutions, stock certificates, organizational minutes. For VC-funded or stock-option growth paths.
