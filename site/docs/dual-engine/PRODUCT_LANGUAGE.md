# DocketMint Product Language

## Use These Phrases

1. One platform. Two operational engines.
2. Legal Workflow.
3. Recovery Pipeline.
4. Shared records.
5. Shared canonical objects.
6. Environment chooser.
7. Capability module.
8. Unified workspace.
9. Escalation continuity.
10. Role-aware operating surface.

## Avoid These Phrases

1. Generic CRM
2. Separate generator app
3. Second product line
4. Dual SaaS
5. Independent Legal database
6. Independent Recovery database
7. Separate identity stack
8. Separate billing stack

## Copy Rules

1. When describing the product, lead with DocketMint as one platform.
2. When describing the environments, name both Legal Workflow and Recovery Pipeline together unless context is strictly single-environment.
3. When describing generation capabilities, tie them to a workflow goal such as packet generation, forms autofill, or document assembly inside DocketMint.
4. When describing post-login behavior, say users enter the environment chooser unless they only have one allowed environment.

## One-Sentence Answer

`DocketMint is one platform with two operational engines, Legal Workflow and Recovery Pipeline, built on shared records, identity, billing, and audit history.`

## Correct Versus Incorrect

Correct:

`DocketMint gives teams one workspace across Legal Workflow and Recovery Pipeline.`

Incorrect:

`Deadline CRM handles one side while a separate generator app handles the rest.`

Correct:

`Packet generation is a capability module inside DocketMint.`

Incorrect:

`The generator is its own product surface.`
