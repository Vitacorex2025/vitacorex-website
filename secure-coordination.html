Step 1 · Contact

Named-contact introduction.

Coordination starts with a named-contact thread, not a generic inbox. This keeps later controls — access grants, notifications, revocations — tied to a specific person who can sign on your side.

  • Open the thread through structured intake or the private consultation page. A named contact on your side is required — title, email, direct phone.
  • Your first response back comes from the director’s line, not from a shared team alias. Replies stay on that thread for the life of the engagement.
  • If the engagement has a Covered Entity or regulated-customer scope, flag it in the first message so the BAA/DPA path starts in parallel with the NDA.
  • No client data is requested, transmitted, or accepted before NDA execution. If a document is attached prematurely, it is returned unopened and you are asked to resend after signing.
Step 2 · NDA

NDA sequencing — mutual or unilateral.

The NDA is the gate to every later step. We keep our template short, mutual, and redline-friendly.

Hour 0–4

Template sent

Our mutual-NDA template (two pages, plain-English) is sent to the named contact, or your template is acknowledged and a redline window is set.

Day 1–3

Redline & signature

Typical redline turnaround is one business day. We accept DocuSign, Adobe Sign, or a PDF with wet-ink signatures from the named contact on each side.

On signature

Portal provisioning starts

Once the NDA is executed, portal provisioning (Step 4) begins the same business day. The countersigned PDF is returned to your named contact for your file.

Unilateral path

Your template accepted

Procurement teams with an enterprise NDA template: send yours; we sign your template directly where the terms are reasonable, or we redline once and return.

Step 3 · BAA / DPA

When BAA or DPA is executed — and when it is not needed.

BAA and DPA are not default-on. They attach when the engagement scope triggers them.

BAA — healthcare engagements with PHI in our environment

A Business Associate Agreement is executed before any Protected Health Information crosses into VitaCoreX infrastructure. Where a healthcare engagement can be run inside your environment (our preferred pattern), a BAA is not required because PHI does not leave your systems — we operate as authorized personnel under your own HIPAA program.

DPA — engagements with regulated consumer / customer data

A Data Processing Addendum attaches to the Master Services Agreement where engagement scope includes GLBA-covered customer data, CCPA / CPRA consumer data, or equivalent state categories. The DPA text is published in summary form on the Sub-processors & DPA page; the full exhibit is countersigned alongside the MSA.

Neither required — corporate / operational data only

For engagements that do not touch PHI or regulated consumer data — corporate contracts, vendor paperwork, internal recovery workflows — the NDA plus MSA is sufficient. We do not paper in a BAA or DPA where scope does not justify it; doing so would misrepresent what the engagement actually covers.

Scope change during engagement

If scope expands mid-engagement into a category that triggers BAA or DPA, work is paused, the addendum is executed, and work resumes. Scope changes are never quietly absorbed.

Step 4 · Portal

Portal provisioning & channel selection.

The channel we use depends on the engagement’s sensitivity tier. Matched to the work, not marketed as one-size-fits-all.

Tier A
Operator environment
We work inside your systems — data never leaves
Tier B
Dedicated secure portal
Engagement-scoped folder, named-user + MFA, AES-256 at rest
Tier C
Encrypted email
TLS 1.2+, attachment-level encryption, limited-size matters
Tier D
SFTP / courier drop
Where a specific counterparty workflow requires it
  • Named-user provisioning: every portal account is tied to a specific person on your side. No shared logins. MFA is mandatory and enforced at login, not optional.
  • Access is scoped to the engagement folder only — no visibility into other matters, no access to administrative or billing data.
  • Activity is logged (upload, download, view, share attempt, failed auth). Log excerpts are available on written request for the duration of the engagement.
  • Tier A (operator environment) is the preferred posture for healthcare, GLBA, and multi-operator matters — it removes the question of where data lives because it lives in your systems throughout.
Step 5 · File exchange

Per-matter file handling.

What happens to a file between the moment you upload it and the moment we deliver the signed output.

On upload

Staging folder, checksum captured

Every upload lands in a staging folder inside the engagement’s dedicated space. A checksum (SHA-256) is captured at ingest and stored with the file so tampering would be detectable.

Within 1 business day

Triage & working-folder move

The engagement lead triages uploads against the file manifest, moves accepted items to the working folder, and flags anything missing or out of scope on the thread.

During work

Versioned, not overwritten

Deliverable drafts live as versioned files (v1, v2, v-final) so the audit trail is preserved. Original source documents are never modified — only copies are worked on.

On delivery

Signed artifact + manifest

Deliverables are released with a signed artifact and a manifest listing what was produced, what source files it derived from, and the delivery date. See Step 7 for chain-of-custody detail.

Uploads over 200 MB: we prefer SFTP or a one-time encrypted-link drop rather than email attachments. The staging-folder contract is identical.

Step 6 · During engagement

Coordination cadence.

Status updates, clarification channel, and change-control expectations during active work.

  • Weekly written status: a short note on what advanced, what is pending, what is blocked, and the expected next-week movement. Delivered on the named-contact thread.
  • Clarification channel: a single thread per engagement (the same one the NDA was sent on). Phone for urgent items, always followed by a written summary back to the thread.
  • Change-control: scope changes are documented in writing (not verbally accepted), with a restated deliverable and any fee adjustment before work resumes.
  • Director reachability: the director is reachable directly during the engagement for any compliance, privacy, or scope concern — not routed through an account manager.
Step 7 · Delivery

Chain of custody on final delivery.

How signed deliverables leave our environment and land in yours, with an audit trail you can file.

  • Final deliverables are released through the same channel as Step 4 — operator environment, portal, encrypted email, or SFTP depending on the engagement tier.
  • Each release includes a signed cover artifact: what is being delivered, on what date, to which named contact, signed by the director.
  • A delivery manifest lists every file produced, its checksum, its size, and the source files it was derived from. Procurement can reconcile deliverables against the intake file list without emailing us.
  • Receipt confirmation is requested from the named contact within 5 business days. If not received, a reminder is sent; the engagement does not close until confirmation is logged.
  • If a deliverable needs clarification after release, the 30-day stabilization window (see Step 8) keeps us reachable without a new engagement scope.
Step 8 · After delivery

Retention, destruction, and post-engagement contact.

What happens to your files after we deliver — and for how long we stay reachable.

Day 0–30

Stabilization window

We stay reachable for clarification questions on the delivered artifact for 30 days post-close, on the same named-contact thread, at no additional fee. Files remain in place during this window.

Day 30–90

Cold storage

Files move to encrypted cold storage. Access is no longer possible from the engagement portal; retrieval requires a written request from the named contact and an engagement-lead action.

Day 90

Scheduled destruction

Default: destruction on day 90 after engagement close, unless extended retention is requested in writing. Invoices and tax records retained 7 years per US standard — file contents are not.

Early — 7-day clock

Destruction on request

A written early-destruction request accelerates the clock to 7 days. A certificate of destruction is issued: files destroyed, method used, date of action, signed by the director.

Operator-environment (Tier A) matters

Access revocation only

Where we worked inside your systems, there is nothing on our side to destroy. At engagement close, our access is revoked; the data path was yours throughout.

Compliance contact

Point of contact for privacy, security, or compliance questions.

Post-engagement or mid-engagement compliance questions — data subject requests, breach-coordination, audit assists — route through the director’s line and are acknowledged within one business day.

  • Director-level intake: compliance questions are not routed through an account manager. The director acknowledges within one business day and assigns a response owner.
  • Data subject requests (CCPA / CPRA / VCDPA / etc.): VitaCoreX supports your response to the consumer. The operator owns the consumer-facing relationship; we do not receive DSR traffic directly.
  • Breach coordination: if an incident potentially affects your engagement data, written notice goes to your named contact within 72 hours of confirmed scope. See the Security & Compliance page, Section 5, for the full incident-response timeline.
  • Audit assists: if your internal or external audit requires a walkthrough of what happened during a closed engagement, the director participates directly on request, subject to the retention window for the specific matter.
Honest footnotes

Stated plainly.

This page describes the operational reality, not marketing. A few qualifiers procurement should weigh when scoring us against larger vendors.

  • Firm size: VitaCoreX is a focused team. Steps 1–8 above are executed by the director and the assigned engagement lead — not by a tiered support desk. That is a feature for most engagements and a limit for extremely high-volume ones. Ask us about concurrency if your matter is wide.
  • Certifications: we are not SOC 2, ISO 27001, or HITRUST certified. The controls on the Security & Compliance page map to those frameworks’ control areas so procurement can score us against them, but we do not claim audits we have not completed.
  • No sub-agents on first contact: your first thread is with the director. Engagement-lead assignment happens after NDA execution — you know who you are working with before you upload anything.
  • Legal boundary: VitaCoreX is not a law firm. Legal strategy remains with your licensed counsel. This coordination protocol describes documentation and workflow posture, not legal advice on your obligations.
