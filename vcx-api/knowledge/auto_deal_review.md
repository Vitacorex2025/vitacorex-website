# Auto Deal Review

This knowledge file is for buyer-side review of dealer numbers before signing. It is not lending, tax, or legal representation.

## Numbers to collect first
Collect cash price, out-the-door price, down payment, trade-in allowance, trade payoff, APR, term length, doc fee, GAP, service contract, add-ons, and estimated taxes/registration. Without these numbers the review stays vague.

## Issues that deserve questions
Large add-ons, unclear products folded into financing, unexplained doc fees, payment quotes without full APR/term disclosure, and trade-in math that hides negative equity should all trigger follow-up questions.

## Good workflow question to ask the user
Ask whether they have a buyer's order, retail installment contract, lease worksheet, or only a sales quote. Route them to upload the strongest document they have.

## Clean escalation path
If the user has the actual dealer paperwork, route them to Auto Deal Check. If they only have a verbal quote or the matter is already in dispute, route to structured intake.
