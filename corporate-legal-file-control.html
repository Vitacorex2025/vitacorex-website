Corporate Legal File Control

Fix intake, chronology, exhibits, and packet quality before outside review begins.

This service is for companies that already have a live matter, document-heavy dispute, or counsel-review need—but do not want to overpay for avoidable cleanup.

Who it is for

Owners, operators, finance teams, legal operations stakeholders, and companies that need a cleaner handoff to counsel or a more controlled internal file before escalation.

Core problem

Most legal waste starts before strategy. Facts are scattered, chronology is unclear, exhibits are duplicated or missing, and questions arrive only after outside review is already underway.

Deliverables

Buyer-facing outputs, not vague “support.”

Matter chronology cleanup

Normalize dates, event order, document references, and issue sequencing.

Exhibit ordering logic

Create a usable exhibit index and control duplicate or missing support.

Issue summary memo

Summarize the problem, pressure points, gaps, and handoff questions.

File map

Show where key records sit, what is missing, and what needs to be requested.

Counsel-ready packet

Assemble a cleaner review package before outside legal cost expands.

Structured handoff notes

Reduce attorney waste by giving licensed counsel a controlled file, not raw chaos.

Compliance posture

The offering is administrative support, file control, packet quality, and documentation discipline. It is not legal advice or legal representation. Strategy, pleadings, and representation remain with licensed counsel where required.
