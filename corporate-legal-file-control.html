Corporate legal file control

Make the file cleaner before review or escalation.

This page covers chronology cleanup, exhibit order, missing-document logs, and packet organization. It does not offer legal advice.

Where companies lose time

  • Chronology spread across multiple folders.
  • Missing authorizations or payment support.
  • Exhibits out of order.
  • No clear issue summary or gap memo.

What the work product should look like

  • A cleaner chronology.
  • Organized exhibits.
  • A missing-items list.
  • A simple next-step memo for internal review or counsel handoff.
