VitaCoreX lead capture setup

Files included:
1. healthcare-cfo_email_capture.html
2. vitacorex_lead_capture.gs
3. PDFFFFFF WEB VitaCoreX Lead Magnet Healthcare Leakage.pdf

Setup steps:
1. Create a Google Sheet.
2. Open Extensions -> Apps Script.
3. Paste vitacorex_lead_capture.gs into Apps Script.
4. Replace SPREADSHEET_ID with your Google Sheet ID.
5. Deploy as Web App. Access: Anyone with the link.
6. Copy the deployed Web App URL.
7. Open healthcare-cfo_email_capture.html and replace:
   PASTE_YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_WEBAPP_URL_HERE
   with your deployed URL.
8. Upload healthcare-cfo_email_capture.html to your site as healthcare-cfo.html.
9. Upload the PDF into the same folder.

What gets collected:
- timestamp
- name
- email
- company
- title
- source_page
- asset_name

Tracking:
- pushes dataLayer event: lead_magnet_download
- fires gtag event if gtag exists
