What we do

Evidence organization, not legal strategy

Clean documentation is the difference between a packet that moves and one that stalls. We organize evidence so your attorney (or you, pro se) can file with confidence.

Evidence indexing

Every document tagged, labeled, and cross-referenced against the checklist on your USCIS form instructions.

Translation review

Foreign-language documents paired with certified translations, logged in the evidence index. Bilingual coordination in EN / RU / ES.

Counsel-ready packet

Formatted tabs, cover sheet, and summary index so your immigration attorney can review and sign off quickly.

Gap log

Missing or weak evidence flagged with plain-English notes so you know what to gather before filing.

FAQ

What families and petitioners usually ask first

Is this immigration legal advice?

No. VitaCoreX LLC is not a law firm. We organize documentation and prepare packets. Petition strategy and filings remain with you or your licensed immigration attorney.

Will you file my USCIS forms?

No. You or your licensed immigration attorney file. We prepare the packet and evidence index so filing is clean and organized.

Do you offer bilingual support?

Yes. Documentation and intake are available in English, Russian, and Spanish. Translation of foreign-language evidence is coordinated as part of the packet.

Which USCIS forms do you support?

Documentation packets for I-130, I-485, N-400, and other common USCIS filings. We do not advise on which form to file.

Can you refer an immigration attorney?

No. We coordinate with counsel you engage but do not provide attorney referrals.
